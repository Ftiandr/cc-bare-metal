SDL - SDL2 for this system (zlib license)

  cc game.c $(sdl2-config --cflags --libs) -o game
  c++ app.cpp -I/usr/include/SDL2 -lSDL2 -o app      (-lSDL2 implies -pthread)

SDL 2.32 with a driver for CC: Bare Metal, so programs written for SDL build
as they are. Programs with SDL use threads (its audio and timers have their
own); they are built for wasm32-wasip1-threads.

Video   A window is the whole screen in 32-bit true colour, at most the
        screen's size (1280x800), or a pixel monitor: SDL_CCBAREMETAL_FB=fb1.
        SDL_CCBAREMETAL_DEPTH=16 (RGB565) or 8 (256 colours, dithered):
        less to send to the players than 32.
        SDL's software renderer: SDL_Renderer, textures (also YUV), window
        surfaces. No OpenGL.
Input   Keys from /dev/input/event0, the mouse from event1 (in pixels of the
        window), typed text (SDL_TEXTINPUT) from the terminal in any keyboard
        layout. Ctrl+C becomes SDL_QUIT.
Audio   SDL's OSS driver on /dev/dsp (the first speaker; AUDIODEV=/dev/dsp1
        for another). Any format, rate and channel count: the kernel
        converts (see 'man dev'). No speaker: the audio plays into silence
        at the right pace (SDL's dummy driver) instead of failing.
Also    threads, mutexes, timers, SDL_GetTicks, SDL_Delay, events, surfaces,
        BMP files. Not here: joysticks, haptics, sensors, shared objects.

Files: /usr/include/SDL2 (headers), /usr/lib/wasm32-wasip1-threads/libSDL2.a,
/usr/bin/sdl2-config. Source: https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL (release
2.32.10); the driver is part of CC: Bare Metal.
