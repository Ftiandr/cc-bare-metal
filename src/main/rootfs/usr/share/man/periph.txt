PERIPH - CC peripherals

  periph list                       attached peripherals
  periph methods NAME               methods of a peripheral
  periph call NAME METHOD [ARGS]    call a method, e.g.
                                    periph call left getItemDetail 1
  periph lua CODE [ARGS]            run Lua with the CC APIs

Peripherals from every mod that supports CC: Tweaked work here: monitors,
modems (wired networks too), disk drives (mounted at /disk), inventories,
Advanced Peripherals, and so on.

POCKET COMPUTER MODULES
On a WASM pocket computer the peripherals are its modules: the "Modules"
button of its window opens 4 slots that take any pocket upgrade of CC or an
addon (speaker, modems, Advanced Peripherals' player detector, geo scanner...).
Slot 1 is "back" (like the upgrade of a CC pocket computer), slots 2-4 are
"slot2", "slot3", "slot4". Putting a module in or taking it out attaches or
detaches it at once (events "peripheral" / "peripheral_detach"). Shift + right
click with a module in the other hand installs it without opening the window.
There is no redstone on a pocket computer.
